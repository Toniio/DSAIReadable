docs: The Marker spec sends every step of the assistant's work to `Trace`, a finished one with nothing to open included, and keeps `Marker` for the events that are not a step (a date, a resumed session, someone who joined): its role no longer names a tool call, and its examples mark a resumed conversation instead of a search. The Code section of the Typography foundation says a block of code is a `CodeBlock`, in one color, and keeps `<code>` for code inside a sentence. The Motion foundation lists the Trace details and the running Trace title under reduced motion, and the Elevation foundation lists the `z-10` of floating `CodeBlockActions`. The target audit of the Size foundation lists `CodeBlockCopyButton` (24px) and the Trace triggers, 16px tall, which meet WCAG 2.5.8 through the spacing exception of the `gap-2` between the steps.
