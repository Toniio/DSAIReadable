mcp: Two answers of the MCP server stop repeating rules that another answer serves, and the detailed design rules come grouped by foundation, since every turn of an agent sends each answer it read again.

- `dsaireadable_get_design_rules` `detailed` without a category serves the foundation rules in a `foundation_rules` object keyed by the foundation's name (`"color": ["…", …]`), in place of `general_rules`, a list where each rule carried its own `"source": "<foundation>.md"`. The answer goes from 36,879 to 30,237 characters, and from 118 foundation rules to 89, of 10 foundations, for the reasons below. A foundation's `category` answer and `ds://guidelines` keep the flat list of `{ rule, source }`. Re-served on the six 0.3.0 runs with the MCP server (claude-sonnet-5-5, with and without the skills), the grouping alone makes the answer 7 to 8 % lighter and cuts the estimated median input tokens per screen by 2,000 to 3,200 per run.
- It leaves out the 14 rules of `voice-and-tone` and `content`, which `dsaireadable_get_ux_writing_rules` serves, and names that tool in its `detail` line. Both foundations stay in `categories`, and a `category` still returns their rules. In the 0.3.0 evals, 76 of the 102 sessions that read this answer called `dsaireadable_get_ux_writing_rules` too.
- It leaves out the 5 rules of `radius.md`, which restate the critical styling rule served whole beside them; the `radius` category still returns them. The styling rule now gives content the scale `radius.md` gives it, `rounded-sm` … `rounded-4xl`, and says `rounded-xs` draws the Chart's indicators: it gave content `rounded-xs` … `rounded-4xl`.
- It serves the foundation rules without the `**` around a numbered rule's title. The category answers and `ds://guidelines` keep it.
- Every answer that serves the foundation rules drops 11 of them: the 6 steps of the design system's upkeep and 3 rules said twice, and the rules on reserved tokens name the class a screen writes, in 2 fewer lines (the `docs` entry of 0500b6b lists them). Size gains one, the `gap-2` between the steps of a `Trace`.
- The `response_format` description and the concise `detail` line no longer promise every foundation's rules: `detailed` adds "the other foundation rules".
- `dsaireadable_get_component_specs` `detailed` serves in `composition_rules` the rules about the component that its `usage` does not already carry: a choice rule (rule-20 to rule-23) is copied into the usage of each spec it covers, and 23 specs served it twice in one answer. The 65 detailed specs went from 357,940 to 337,140 characters.
- A component's composition rules, in that answer and in `dsaireadable_get_design_rules` with the component's name as `category`, are the rules whose `applies_to` lists it, or, for a rule without `applies_to`, whose text names it in its case. Matched in any case, 9 components got a rule about something else: Button got rule-13, the `data-slot` authoring rule, for `data-slot='button'` (`category: "Button"` went from 1,068 to 853 characters), and Message got rule-09 for "an error message". A foundation's name still matches in any case: `spacing` returns rule-12.
