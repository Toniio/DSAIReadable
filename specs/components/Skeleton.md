## Role

An animated loading placeholder in the shape of the content to come, which makes waiting feel shorter.

## Usage

- Show a skeleton while asynchronous data loads
- Stand in for text, images or cards until they render
- Build full page skeletons by combining several `Skeleton`s
- Preview a list's structure (`SidebarMenuSkeleton`, for example)
- Spare the user blank screens and spinners

## Constraints

- **MUST NOT** — stay on screen once the content has loaded
- **MUST** — match the final content's dimensions (height, width, radius), so its arrival does not shift the layout
- **MUST NOT** — animate a skeleton while `prefers-reduced-motion` is on
- **MUST NOT** — show more skeletons than the entries expected: each one holds the place of content that is coming

## Accessibility

**Pattern**: None — a visual placeholder

**Role**: A `div` with no role.

**Keyboard**:

No interaction.

**Accessible name**: Not applicable.

**Pitfalls**:

- Announce the loading elsewhere: `aria-busy="true"` on the container, or a `Spinner` (which carries `role="status"`).
- Skeletons mean nothing to a screen reader: hide their container (`aria-hidden`) when there are many.
