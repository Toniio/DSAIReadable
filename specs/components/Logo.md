## Role

The application's brand mark. The component renders the pictogram alone; it is a placeholder, to be swapped for the real brand assets once they are provided.

## Usage

- The header of the sign-in page
- Primary navigation (sidebar, top bar)
- The footer

## Constraints

- **MUST** — show the logo through the `Logo` component, never through a hand-inserted SVG
- **MUST NOT** — distort its proportions: go through `size`
- **MUST** — place the brand name next to the `Logo`: it does not render its children
- **MUST** — replace this placeholder with the real brand assets as soon as they exist

## Dependencies

- `cn` (`@/lib/utils`) to merge classes
- No external dependency — the pictogram is an internal SVG

## Accessibility

**Pattern**: Decorative image

**Role**: The SVG pictogram carries `aria-hidden="true"`.

**Keyboard**:

No interaction of its own.

**Accessible name**: None: the logo is decorative. The brand name must come from nearby text, or from the `aria-label` of the link that wraps it ("Home — BankApp").

**Pitfalls**:

- A logo alone in an unnamed link produces an empty link: always name the link.
